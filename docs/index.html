Brand

The Operator is the lock-screen avatar: 48×48 pixel art, scaled by whole multiples only. It recolours with the theme.

Color

One 12-step phosphor ramp per theme; every step has one job. Status hues are the only extra colour, and always come with a tag.

Label · PxPlus 16 uppercase

Body text is PxPlus IBM VGA 8x16 at its native 16px, with a 24px line for prose. Pixel fonts have no bold: emphasis is brighter, or underlined, or inverted. Links look like this.

Secondary text, hints and placeholders use step 8 and still meet 4.5:1.

Small text is PxPlus 8x14 at 14px. Code is PxPlus 9x16; keys are Ctrl+Alt+M.
