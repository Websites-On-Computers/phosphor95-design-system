# Imagery

Phosphor95 imagery is **light on a dark screen**: digital rain, scanlines, dither, POST text and the
Operator. No photography and no gradients beyond phosphor glow. Reference images are in `imagery/`.

| Image | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Digital rain wallpaper | `imagery/phosphor95-rain-1440x900.png` | Generated by the theme (`../theme/tools/components/wallpaper.py`) |
| Boot splash | `imagery/boot-logo.png` | Wordmark, tagline, segmented progress, passphrase dialog |
| POST screen | `imagery/boot-post.png` | BIOS-style boot lines |

## Motifs

| Motif | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Digital rain** | Background only: desktops, splash, lock and empty hero areas. **Never behind body text.** Density is `effect.rain-density`, with half-katakana and digits in `color.phosphor.6–8`, and only the leading glyph at `color.phosphor.12`. Static under reduced motion |
| **Scanlines** | A texture over whole screens or hero images (`effect.scanline-opacity`, every `effect-size.scanline-period`). Not on small components |
| **Dither** | 50% checkerboard of two adjacent ramp steps. It's the Operator's shading, the scrollbar track and the disabled overlay. Never mix non-adjacent steps |
| **POST / boot text** | Uppercase mono lines with status tags, typed at `duration.type` per character. Good for splash screens and long jobs |
| **Segmented bars** | 8px blocks with 2px gaps (`progress.*`). Used for progress, meters and the boot loader |
| **Bevel windows** | Win95 chrome in phosphor. A single window on a rain field is the signature composition |

## Composition

- **One bright thing.** Each screen has one `color.phosphor.12` focal point (cursor, wordmark, leading rain
  glyph, active title). Everything else sits at steps 6–10.
- **Dark first.** At least 70% of a composition is `surface.void`/`surface.base`.
- **On the grid.** Every element snaps to the 8×16 cell, including text in images.
- **Status colour is rare.** Amber and red appear in imagery only when they mean warn or error.

## Making new imagery

Generate it from tokens: the theme's PIL tools and `tools/brand.py` show the pattern (read colours via
`load_context()`, draw at 1x, upscale with nearest neighbour). Don't hand-pick hex values.
